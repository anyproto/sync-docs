---
title: Future work
description: The roadmap for any-sync — global P2P relays, multi-provider support, and nested ACLs.
---

The future development of `any-sync` focuses on three key directions:

1. **Global Peer-to-Peer Relays**: Enable seamless direct synchronization over the internet by addressing connectivity challenges, ensuring reliable peer-to-peer communication in all scenarios.
2. **Multi-Provider Support**: Introduce flexibility and resilience by allowing users to connect with multiple sync providers, reducing dependency on a single provider while enhancing performance and reliability.
3. **Nested Access Control Lists (ACLs)**: Implement advanced, hierarchical permission management, empowering channel owners with precise control over access rights and fostering secure collaboration at every level.
